## 📊 **Launch Strategy**

### **Timing**
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday
- **Best time:** 12:01 AM PST (Product Hunt resets at midnight Pacific)
- **Why:** Gives you full 24 hours to climb the leaderboard

### **Pre-Launch (1 day before)**
1. Create Product Hunt "Coming Soon" page
2. Build email list of supporters (friends, Twitter followers)
3. Prepare 5 "Hunter" candidates (people with PH reputation to hunt your product)
4. Create all gallery images
5. Write first comment in Google Doc

### **Launch Day (Hour-by-Hour)**

**12:01 AM PST:** Submit product (or have Hunter submit)

**12:05 AM:** Post first comment (the long one above)

**6:00 AM:** Check rankings, respond to ALL comments

**8:00 AM:** Tweet about Product Hunt launch:
```
🚀 We're LIVE on Product Hunt!

Zero-Knowledge Vault with passwordless passkey login is now available.

Show some love ❤️
→ https://producthunt.com/posts/[your-product-slug]

#ProductHunt #LaunchDay
```

**10:00 AM - 2:00 PM:** Peak engagement hours
- Respond to every comment within 5 minutes
- Upvote other products (karma = visibility)
- Share in relevant communities

**4:00 PM:** Check leaderboard position
- If top 5: Push harder on social media
- If below top 5: Rally your network for final push

**8:00 PM:** Final push tweet:
```
4 hours left! We're #[X] on @ProductHunt 🎉

If you care about privacy and passwordless security, we'd love your support!

→ https://producthunt.com/posts/[your-product-slug]
```

**11:59 PM PST:** Day ends, rankings freeze

## 💡 **Tips for Success**

### **Getting to Top 5:**
1. **Hunter matters:** Someone with PH reputation can boost initial visibility
2. **First hour is critical:** Get 20-30 upvotes in first hour
3. **Respond to EVERYTHING:** High engagement = algorithm boost
4. **Cross-promote:** Twitter, LinkedIn, email list, Discord servers
5. **Hunt on Tuesday/Wednesday:** Less competition than Monday

### **What NOT to Do:**
❌ Ask for upvotes directly (against PH rules)
❌ Use bots or fake accounts (instant ban)
❌ Spam communities
❌ Ignore comments (kills engagement)
❌ Launch on Friday (low traffic)

### **Engagement Hacks:**
✅ Ask questions in your first comment ("What would YOU store?")
✅ Post updates throughout the day ("Just hit 100 upvotes! 🎉")
✅ Show appreciation ("Thanks @username for the kind words!")
✅ Be authentic and personal (not corporate-speak)

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the request limit?

Your monthly request limit covers calls to the /api/haikus endpoint. Rate limits are enforced per minute to prevent abuse. Unused requests do not roll over to the next month.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes! You can change your plan anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. Unused balance is credited.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and debit cards via Stripe. All payments are processed securely with PCI compliance.

Is there a free trial?

Contact us at support@example.com to discuss trial options. We're happy to set up a test account for evaluation.

What happens if I exceed my limit?

Requests that exceed your rate limit receive a 429 (Too Many Requests) response. We recommend monitoring your usage via the admin dashboard and upgrading if needed.
